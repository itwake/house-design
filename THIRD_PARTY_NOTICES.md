## Blender

The V3 `.blend`, `.glb`, procedural surface textures and room images are generated by `tools/build_blender.py` using Blender 4.5.9 LTS. Blender itself is not bundled in this repository. The render images come from the same modeled scene as the browser asset.

The V3.1 material branches are generated non-destructively by `tools/build_design_schemes.py`, including their procedural wood, fabric, stone, normal maps and small geometric book-cover graphics. Their room images are actual Blender renders, not edited photographs from reference projects.
